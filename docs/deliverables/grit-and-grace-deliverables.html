What we're delivering

Grit & Grace Digital Academy — the Inner Court, online and in your pocket

1
Your website
Story, programs, events, journal and gallery — edit it yourself, no developer. Parents enrol and pay by mobile money or card; mid-year pricing is automatic. One inbox for every enquiry.
2
The Inner Court
Her own space: monthly video lessons, workbooks, a private journal, badges and certificates anyone can verify. Data-saver mode keeps video light on mobile bundles.
3
A safe sisterhood
Private Circles with their mentor, Wins of the Week, prayer and book chats. Only kindness: no dislikes, no strangers, closes at 9 pm. Every post checked; “Talk to someone” any time.
4
Live sessions
Circle mentor hours with an audio-only option for low data, whole-Academy broadcasts, and replays afterwards.
5
Parent portal
Progress, attendance, certificates and her growth across the five values. Parents choose what she can access and pay instalments. Her journal stays hers.
6
Mobile app
The whole experience on iPhone, iPad and Android. The iPhone app is already in testing on Apple's TestFlight.
7
The Palace
Your command centre — no developer needed: upload lessons and videos, schedule live sessions, review posts, place girls in Circles, invite mentors, track payments, see where girls drop off.
8
Safety & privacy
Parent consent first. Community off for under-13s until a parent switches it on. Any sign a girl is at risk alerts you immediately. Built for Ghana's data protection rules.
9
Works with Zoho
Enquiries, enrolments and payments flow into the Zoho tools you already use.
