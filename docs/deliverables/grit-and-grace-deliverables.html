Your website

A modern home for the Academy that you update yourself

  • New pages: About, Programs, Events, Journal, Gallery, Contact
  • Change words, photos and prices from one simple screen
  • Parents enrol online and pay by mobile money or card
  • Mid-year pricing is worked out automatically
  • Event pages with a live countdown
  • Every enquiry lands in one inbox and your email
  • Looks beautiful on phones as well as computers

For you: no more waiting on a developer to change a date or a price.

Online student portal

Her own space to learn, month by month

  • 12 monthly modules built on the G.I.R.L.S. values
  • Video lessons, devotionals and assignments
  • Workbooks to download for each lesson
  • A private journal — only she can read it
  • Badges, crown levels and certificates anyone can verify
  • “Continue where you left off” and progress tracking
  • Data-saver mode to protect her mobile bundle

For her: everything in one place, at her own pace.

3

Online community

A safe sisterhood — not social media

  • A private Circle of 8–12 girls with their own mentor
  • Spaces for Wins of the Week, prayer requests and books
  • Four kind reactions only — Crown, Heart, Praying, Amen
  • No dislike button, no strangers, no followers
  • Every post checked before it appears
  • Quiet hours: closes at 9 pm, opens at 6 am
  • Private messages with her mentor (parents can switch off)

For parents: she belongs somewhere safe.

Live sessions

Meet together, wherever she is

  • Circle mentor hours in video rooms inside the app
  • Audio-only mode when data is low
  • Whole-Academy broadcasts and the speaker series
  • Attendance recorded automatically
  • Replays afterwards — can become a lesson
  • Schedule a session in minutes from the dashboard

For mentors: no links to chase — girls just tap “Join”.

Safety & privacy

Built in from the start, not added later

  • Parents give consent first — her account comes after
  • A permanent record of every consent
  • Community off for under-13s until a parent says yes
  • Instant alerts if a girl may be at risk, or a stranger reaches out
  • “Talk to someone” reaches a real adult privately
  • Built for Ghana's Data Protection Act
  • No ads, and her data is never sold

For everyone: peace of mind.
